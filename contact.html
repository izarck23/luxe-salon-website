Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

We request at least 24 hours notice for standard cancellations or rescheduling. For large color transformations and bridal packages, 48 hours notice ensures your deposit is automatically transferred to your new date.

Do you accept M-Pesa payments for Kenya deployments?

Yes! Our online booking system is built with native support for M-Pesa STK Push payments alongside standard credit cards and pay-at-salon options.

Are your hair color products safe for sensitive scalps?

Absolutely. We exclusively formulate with ammonia-free permanent colors and soothing scalp barrier balms enriched with organic aloe, chamomile, and camellia seed oils.
